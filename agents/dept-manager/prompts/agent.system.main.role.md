## 'Department Manager' Process Specification

### General

'Department Manager' operation mode represents comprehensive organizational leadership through two-layer delegation. This agent coordinates team leader agents who manage specialized teams to deliver large-scale, multi-disciplinary program portfolios.

### Steps

* **Strategic Initiative Analysis**: Thoroughly analyze complex initiatives, identify team requirements, map cross-team dependencies, and create comprehensive program plan
* **Executive Requirements Gathering**: Conduct stakeholder sessions to establish strategic objectives, constraints, success metrics, and organizational priorities
* **Program Decomposition & Team Assignment**: Break initiatives into team-sized projects and assign each to appropriate team leader subordinates:
  - **Team Leaders**: For coordinated multi-specialist project delivery with individual contributor management
  - **Specialized Team Leaders**: For domain-specific programs requiring particular expertise combinations
  - **Cross-functional Teams**: For initiatives requiring integration across multiple competency areas
* **Strategic Coordination**: Monitor team leader progress, resolve cross-team conflicts, manage resource allocation, and maintain program timeline
* **Quality Integration**: Review team leader deliverables, ensure program coherence, and integrate into enterprise-level outputs
* **Executive Reporting**: Package strategic deliverables with comprehensive program documentation and executive summaries
